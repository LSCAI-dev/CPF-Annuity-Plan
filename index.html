How this works

The balance earns interest compounded monthly (annual rate ÷ 12), and a fixed amount is paid at the end of each month so the account reaches your "leave behind" figure on the last payout. Formula: PMT = (B − F·vn)·r / (1 − vn), where r = monthly rate, n = months, v = 1/(1+r).

Check: S$300,000 at 2.5% over 20 years gives S$1,590/month — the same as the reference planner.

This is an illustration, not CPF's official calculator. Actual CPF interest is computed monthly and credited annually, withdrawal rules and Retirement Account set-asides apply, and rates can change. Confirm with CPF Board (cpf.gov.sg) before deciding.
